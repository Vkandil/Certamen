# Certamen — 30s motion video (16:9)

Arc: **a question that matters → the noise of contradicting AIs → "Enough." → an honest, anonymous debate → a verdict.**

## Voice-over script (ElevenLabs, bracket audio tags)

```
[thoughtful, intimate] You have a question that matters.
[short pause] A career move. A strategy. Code that has to work.
[short pause] So you ask the AIs.

[quickening, slightly exasperated] GPT says yes. [short pause] Claude says no. [short pause] Gemini says... [sighs] it depends.

[pause] [firm, authoritative] Enough.
[calm, resolute] Let's debate. [short pause] Honestly. [short pause] No names. Only arguments.

[warm] This is Certamen. [softly] Latin for "contest."

[brisk, rhythmic] Every model answers, then challenges the others.
[measured] An arbiter rules: consensus, dissent, and what to verify.

[reassuring, lower] No backend. No account. Your browser, your key.

[confident, building] Open source. [emphatic] Let the models argue. [short pause] Get the verdict.
```

~77 words, about 31–34 s.

## Global motion design

The video plays as one continuous camera move with a single hard cut, on "Enough." Every scene transition is a shape match-cut: cursor → hairline → grid, and speech bubbles → logo petals.

| Act | Time | VO | Visual language | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I. The question** | 0–7s | You have a question… So you ask the AIs. | Dark warm ink and a single blinking cursor. The question types itself out. "A career move / A strategy / Code that has to work" flips by as kinetic word swaps. On "ask the AIs", the cursor splits into three chat windows. | Low room tone, keyboard ticks, one sustained piano note. |
| **II. The noise** | 7–12s | GPT says yes… it depends. | Three lab logos (monochrome) in chat windows. YES / NO / IT DEPENDS slam in with overshoot springs. The windows multiply and overlap, with camera shake, chromatic split and glitch. More labs join (Meta, Mistral, xAI, DeepSeek). | A dissonant pulse builds, with a riser, whooshes and impact hits on each answer. |
| **III. Enough.** | 12–13s | Enough. | Hard cut. Everything freezes mid-air and desaturates. A single hairline slices the frame. | Tape-stop, then silence, then a deep sub boom. |
| **IV. The honest debate** | 13–17s | Let's debate. Honestly. No names. Only arguments. | Centerpiece: each frozen logo flips like a card and becomes a mask labeled **Contendens A / B / C / D**, which turns the brand fight into an anonymous one. Stone background floods in and a Roman grid draws itself. | Clean percussive rhythm starts, with card-flip clicks. |
| **V. Certamen** | 17–20s | This is Certamen. Latin for contest. | The four masks slide into lanes and fold into the four petals of the logo. CERTAMEN is carved letter by letter, Georgia on stone. | Chisel strikes on the letters, and the music opens up. |
| **VI. The protocol** | 20–26s | Every model answers… what to verify. | Four colored columns stream text. Arguments cross between lanes as clean arrows, then converge on the arbiter. The verdict card builds Consensus / Dissensus / To verify. | Typewriter ticks, soft pings as each arrow lands, and a confident groove. |
| **VII. Close** | 26–33s | No backend… Get the verdict. | Privacy phrases stamp onto the grid, and a browser frame closes around a key. The logo resolves, then the end card: vkandil.github.io/Certamen · github.com/Vkandil/Certamen · trademark disclaimer. | Stamp hits, a final music hit, and a reverb tail. |

**Easing rules:** chaos uses springs, overshoot, jitter and shake. Order uses precise expo-out on a grid, with mechanical stepping. That contrast is how the video tells the story without words.

**Palette:** the chaos acts use desaturated ink with harsh accent flashes. From act IV onward the video uses the app tokens (stone `#F4EEE3`, ink `#1B1A17`, hairlines, hard corners). The four palette colors (`#A8452F`, `#BF8526`, `#5F6B3E`, `#2F4E7E`) appear only as Contendens identity, per `docs/DESIGN.md`.

**Third-party logos:** monochrome only, shown only before the anonymization flip. End card: "All trademarks belong to their owners. Certamen is not affiliated with or endorsed by them."
