# Certamen — 30s motion video

Concept: **chaos → order.** The AI labs argue loudly and contradict each other. Certamen arrives, freezes the brawl, and turns it into a structured debate with a verdict.

## Voice-over script (ElevenLabs, bracket audio tags)

```
[playful, slightly exasperated] GPT says yes. [short pause] Claude says no. [short pause] Gemini says... [sighs] it depends.
[amused] Everyone's confident. [short pause] Nobody agrees.

[pause] [firm, authoritative] Enough.
[short pause] [warm] This is Certamen. [softly] Latin for "contest."

[brisk, rhythmic] Every model answers. Then they debate: names hidden, arguments only.
[measured] An arbiter rules: consensus, dissent, and what to verify.

[reassuring, lower] No backend. No account. Just your browser, and your key.

[confident, building] Certamen. Open source.
[emphatic] Let the models argue. [short pause] Get the verdict.
```

~60 words, about 28–32 s.

## Storyboard (timed to the voice)

| Time | Line | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | GPT / Claude / Gemini… | Lab logos ricochet across a dark frame. Each fires a speech bubble ("YES." "NO." "IT DEPENDS."). The bubbles collide, throw sparks and shake the camera. |
| 4–7s | Everyone's confident. Nobody agrees. | More labs join (Meta, Mistral, xAI, DeepSeek). Bubbles stack and overlap into noise, with glitch, chromatic split and tempo ramping up. |
| 7–8s | Enough. | Hard cut to silence. Everything freezes mid-air as one hairline rule slices across the screen. |
| 8–11s | This is Certamen. Latin for contest. | Stone background floods in and a Roman grid draws itself. The frozen logos drop into four lanes, and the lanes fold into the four petals of the Certamen logo. CERTAMEN is carved in serif. |
| 11–15s | Every model answers… names hidden | Four columns stream answers. Each logo flips like a card to "Contendens A/B/C/D". Arguments travel between the lanes as clean arrows instead of sparks. |
| 15–20s | An arbiter rules… | The arrows converge on the arbiter. A verdict card builds in three sections: Consensus / Dissensus / To verify. |
| 20–24s | No backend… your key | Kinetic type stamps each phrase. A browser frame closes around a key. |
| 24–30s | Open source. Let the models argue. Get the verdict. | The logo resolves. End card shows vkandil.github.io/Certamen and the GitHub repo, with a trademark disclaimer line. |

Visual system: the chaos phase uses a dark background with loud, clashing colors. The order phase switches to the app's own tokens (stone `#F4EEE3`, ink `#1B1A17`, hairlines, hard corners, Georgia display). After that, the four palette colors appear only as model identity, per `docs/DESIGN.md`.

Third-party logos are shown in monochrome only, as references to the models Certamen can run through OpenRouter. The end card carries: "All trademarks belong to their owners. Certamen is not affiliated with or endorsed by them."
